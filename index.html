📖 ISL Sign Reference Guide (How to perform the signs) ▾

👋 Greetings & Basics

  • HELLO / HI: Open palm facing camera, 5 fingers upright.
  • STOP: Flat upright palm pushed forward.
  • WAIT: Open relaxed palm facing upward.
  • PLEASE / THANK YOU: Flat palm at chest or moving forward.
  • SORRY: Closed fist at chest.

✅ Responses

  • YES / GOOD: 👍 Thumbs Up (fist with thumb upright).
  • NO: 🤏 Pinch Index & Thumb, other 3 fingers curled.
  • OK: 👌 Pinch Index & Thumb into 'O', other 3 fingers extended up.
  • BAD: 🤙 Pinky extended alone pointing down/out, other fingers curled.

💧 Daily Essentials

  • WATER: 🖖 'W' Shape — 3 fingers (Index, Middle, Ring) extended upright, thumb on pinky.
  • FOOD / EAT: 🤌 All 5 fingertips pinched together.
  • DRINK: 🥛 Curved 'C' shape hand like holding a glass.
  • HELP: 🫱 Flat horizontal palm.
  • PHONE: 🤙 'Y' Shape — Thumb & Pinky extended like a receiver.
  • HOME: 🏠 Fingers angled together forming a tent roof.

❓ Pronouns & Questions

  • YOU: 👆 Index finger pointing straight forward at camera.
  • I / ME: Index finger pointing inward toward chest.
  • HOW: ✌️ 'V' Shape — Index & Middle extended spread apart.
  • ARE: 🤘 Horns — Index & Pinky extended, middle 2 curled.
  • LOVE: 🤟 'ILY' — Thumb, Index, & Pinky extended, middle 2 curled.
  • TIME: ⌚ Index pointing to wrist (watch location).
  • SPACE: 🫲 Flat horizontal hand gesture.

📋 Real ISL Collection Protocol

  • 1. Select Sign: Choose the target word from the 30 standardized ISL vocabulary classes.
  • 2. Align Hand: Position hand 50–70 cm from the camera ensuring all 21 joint landmarks are tracked.
  • 3. Pose Naturally: Perform the authentic ISLRTC sign. Vary your hand angle slightly during capture to build model robustness.
  • 4. Genuine Samples: Each captured sample contains genuine 3D coordinates. No synthetic or duplicated frames are saved.
  • 5. Export & Train: Export the recorded samples into dataset/<WORD>/ and retrain with npm run train:v2.
